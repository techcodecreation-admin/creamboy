Cream Boy Fresh Cakes & Bakes

Serving Kumbakonam Since 2010

The Origin Story

Rooted in Tradition, Baked with Pure Heart

Cream Boy Fresh Cakes & Bakes began on Sastra College Road in Kumbakonam with a passionate commitment: to deliver exceptional celebration cakes, pure cow ghee sweets, and authentically spiced South Indian karam using only the freshest ingredients.

Over the past 15 years, our oven doors have opened each morning before dawn. We believe baking is not just about sugar and flour — it is about honoring the milestone moments of our customers' lives.

Our Journey

15 Years of Culinary Milestones

2010

The Beginning

Opened our bakery on Sastra College Road near Sastra University Ground, Anna Nagar.

2015

Pure Ghee Sweets

Expanded into traditional pure cow ghee sweets including Mysore Pak, wheat halwa, and festive ladoos.

2020

Artisan Cake Studio

Introduced custom multi-tier celebration cakes, photo cakes, and dark chocolate truffle designs.

Today

5000+ Celebrations

A trusted household name across Kumbakonam for celebrations, weddings, and festivals.
